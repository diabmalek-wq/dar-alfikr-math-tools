**Source read:** two PDF parts of the export (299 + 312 pages = 611 Hard Math questions, one per page), read as page images so the math was visible. All 611 were transcribed and tagged by archetype.
**Method:** every export item was tagged by skill and archetype, then compared with the bank by keyword and by reading the bank's own items. Bank items are house-authored; **no official item, wording or numbers were copied**. The new items reuse only the archetype.
