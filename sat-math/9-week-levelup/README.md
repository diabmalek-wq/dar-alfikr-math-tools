# SAT Math Mastery — 9-week level-up (private tutoring)

Each session has two PDFs: a **Worksheet** and an **Answers** copy. The Answers copy is identical to the worksheet, with every solution step written in green under each problem.

| Files | What they are |
|---|---|
| `SAT-M_Week-XX_<Title>_*.pdf` | Level-up version: 30 problems (5 Easy, 8 Medium, 10 Hard, 7 SAT Challenge) |
| `SAT-M_Week-XX-Plus_<Title>_*.pdf` | Plus version: all 30 level-up problems kept, plus extra Challenge problems from Trick Pack 1, placed by topic (32 to 48 problems) |

Sessions: 1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 4A, 4B, 5A, 5B, 6A, 6B, 7A, 7B, 8A, 8B, 9A, 9B. Week 1B Plus is the 40-problem version.
