## Problem groups

**1. Matrix diagonalization ($A_1$ to $A_5$).** Topic: diagonalizability of $2\times2$ and $3\times3$ matrices ($A_1$ diagonal, $A_2$ upper triangular with repeated eigenvalue, $A_3$ diagonal with a repeated entry, $A_4=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-2&3\end{pmatrix}$, $A_5$ upper triangular). Task: decide whether each is diagonalizable; for each diagonalizable one, construct an invertible $P$ and diagonal $D$ with $A=PDP^{-1}$.

**2. Diagonalizable linear operators ($T_1$ to $T_6$).** Topic: operators on $\mathbb R^2$, $P_2$ and $M_{2\times2}$, including $T_1(x,y)=(2x,3y)$, $T_2(x,y)=(4x+y,4y)$, $T_3(p)=p'$, $T_4(p)=xp(x)$, $T_5(A)=A^T$, and $T_6$ (multiplication by a fixed matrix [side unclear in scan]). Task: decide diagonalizability; for each diagonalizable operator, construct a basis $\beta$ with $[T]_\beta$ diagonal, and the change-of-basis matrices $[I]_\beta^\alpha$ and $[I]_\alpha^\beta$, where $\alpha$ is the standard basis.

**3. Applying polynomials to matrices.** Topic: $f(x)=3x^2+2x-5$ applied to the diagonalizable matrices from group 1. Task: compute $f(A)$ for each, using $f(A)=Pf(D)P^{-1}$.

**4. Applying polynomials to operators.** Topic: $f(x)=x^2+2x-5$ applied to the diagonalizable operators from group 2. Task: explicitly define $f(T)$ for each.

**5. Proofs (beyond the final's scope).** Topic: (a) the characteristic polynomial $\chi_A(x)$ of $A\in M_{n\times n}(F)$; (b) the equivalence $AP=PD \iff A\vec v_i=\lambda_i\vec v_i$ for all $i$, where $P=[\vec v_1\cdots\vec v_n]$ and $D$ is diagonal. Task: prove each statement.
